Website service

Scoped project available

Give your business a website that has a job to do.

CARBON° helps define and build a website around the business, the people it serves and the action visitors should take. The scope starts with the business need—not a generic template.

Request a website scope

What the project can cover

  • A clear structure for the business and its offer.
  • A proposed page layout and visual direction suited to its customers.
  • Copy and calls to action for enquiry, booking, sales or another agreed goal.
  • Responsive implementation and a published route, where included in the final scope.
  • Further integrations only after their availability, terms and acceptance criteria are confirmed.

How it starts

Send the business name, what it does, its target customers, any existing public links and the result the site should achieve. We will respond with a proposed scope, deliverables, price and next steps before paid work is authorised.

A visual mockup can be included in the agreed proposal where suitable. The automatic paid-demo/trial provisioning flow is not yet available as a self-service feature, so a mockup, interactive demo, trial period or payment step will only be promised when it is explicitly confirmed in the quote.

For the business owner: requesting a scope does not commit you to purchase. We confirm the delivery and commercial terms before asking you to proceed.
