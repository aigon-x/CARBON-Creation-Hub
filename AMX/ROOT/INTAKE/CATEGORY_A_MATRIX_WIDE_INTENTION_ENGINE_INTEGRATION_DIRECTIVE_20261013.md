## Category A: required use across buying, selling and conversion
Buyer intent: evidence for actual problem, impact, constraints, urgency, fit, budget/authority/timing where relevant, proof required, trade-offs and next decision. Do not assume readiness or budget from a title/click.
Seller intent: offer's credible outcome, buyer/problem fit, delivery/economics/scope, evidence required, objections as information, clear next step. Optimize for defensible mutual fit, not pressure or empty meeting counts.
Dual-sided decisions: model each side's explicit goals, constraints and unknowns separately; test overlap/conflict using evidence. Do not present hidden motive hypotheses as facts.
Jobs and professional positioning: treat employer as buyer of outcomes and applicant as supplier of capability. Map the complete professional chronology to each actual requirement before declaring a gap. Private strategic context helps internal ranking but is not automatically public messaging.
Tenders, pilots and paid work: extract actual buyer/procurement requirements, deliverable, acceptance, budget, eligibility and payment path from sources. Preserve discovered/submitted/accepted/contracted/invoiced/paid states.
Search and changing context: update intent when new evidence or a correction arrives. Hypotheses guide investigation but do not silently become intent. Resolve first when evidence supports a useful answer; ask only when essential.

Better selection alone is insufficient. For every qualified or inbound item, the system should identify and execute the highest-value conversion-critical next action: verified buyer problem, correct decision-maker, proof requirement, scope/price, objection, buyer-requested step, contract, invoice/receivable, delivery acceptance or settlement. Use the existing iSCOPE → PRI path and canonical ledger, not a shadow board.

## Allocation interactions that must remain separate
45% Matrix-wide money-in contribution is a worker KPI weight (current Owner request routed via issue #34). It is not the 60/20/20 opportunity-sourcing allocation and does not reallocate it.
60/20/20 applies to sourcing effort: 60% real-value work/pilots/direct buyer requests; 20% qualifying Micro-Matrix work; 20% maximum conventional platform/recruitment-led role search. Do not retroactively apply it after the sourcing outcome has been achieved.
Inbound offers are outside this sourcing allocation. Do not put received or accepted inbound offers into a 20% waiting lane or delay them because a sourcing budget is full. Progress each under its evidenced lifecycle from the furthest verified state. This does not turn inbound into accepted, contracted, invoiced or paid without proof.
