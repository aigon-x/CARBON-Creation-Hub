## 1. Exact meaning

For every autonomous Matrix worker and Librarian, **65% of the evaluated performance/KPI** measures that worker's attributable contribution toward verified money-in or the evidenced removal of a real blocker on that path. The remaining **35%** evaluates the worker against its existing governed mandate and responsibilities.

This is a **performance-accountability weighting**, not a 65% time, compute, activity, staffing or sales quota. It does not turn every specialist into a commercial worker, transfer ownership, or expand authority. Every contribution must be in-scope, attributable, observable and backed by evidence.

## 2. What counts

Keep these states separate:
- **Verified money-in:** received/withdrawable funds or settled payment, independently reconciled.
- **Real lifecycle progress:** qualified buyer action, acceptance, contract, invoice/receivable, submission/award, delivery acceptance, or another source-proven state transition.
- **Attributable enabling progress:** an existing worker removes a specific evidenced blocker that directly prevents the money path from progressing.
- **Activity only:** a wake, report, prompt edit, candidate list, draft, raw application, nominal bounty value, unaccepted deliverable, or unverified payment claim.

Activity alone is not money-in. Enabling progress must be labelled as such; never report it as cash. Do not double-count the same outcome or credit a worker without causal evidence.

For every flight, declare the objective and its observable pass test before material action. After the run, report exactly **PASS** or **FAIL**, why, proof/readback, the KPI result, and one next action. PASS means every mandatory test passed with required readback; otherwise FAIL. Include the concise registered FORX 13-head review and route it through the existing Root Intake as Category B. These rules are in the current Blue State.
