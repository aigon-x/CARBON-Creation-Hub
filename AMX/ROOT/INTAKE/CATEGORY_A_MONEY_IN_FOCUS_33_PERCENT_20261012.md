1. Direct buyer-to-builder paid pilots worldwide: specific buyer problem, short bounded deliverable, clear acceptance test, identifiable payer, budget/credible purchasing route and prompt path to contract/invoice/payment. No unpaid speculative build.
2. Microtask/micro-metrics routes with current work or evidenced assignment path, local eligibility, acceptance/rejection terms, actual available task stock, approval delay, threshold, fees, pay cycle and withdrawable payout method.
3. Legitimate Web3/crypto/community work: official project and contributor communities, open-source tasks, QA/reviews and funded bounties where issuer, authorized scope, current status, acceptance rules and payout are verifiable.
4. Existing warm leads, buyer responses, paid-task completion, invoicing, payment-rail repair and settlement/withdrawal readback.

Deduplicate by real payer, underlying workstream, account gate and payout bottleneck. Several recruitment agencies or AI-generated/syndicated listings from one provider are not several independent opportunities. Keep stages separate: eligible, assigned, accepted task, work accepted, receivable, payment pending, paid, withdrawable. Only the last evidenced stage may be claimed.
