## 1. Direct Owner supersession

The Owner’s latest instruction supersedes the prior 33% request with **45%**. The 33% artifact and previous 25% language remain historical provenance, not the current intended target.

**Current requested KPI:** For **every autonomous Matrix worker, including every Librarian and worker**, 45% of its evaluated performance/KPI is measured against (a) whether attributable money-in outcomes are occurring and (b) what that worker did that materially contributed to verified money-in or removed a specific evidenced blocker on the path to it.

This is a **performance-accountability weight**, not a declaration that 45% of wall-clock time, compute, discovery effort or every office mandate must be spent on commercial activities. Do not conflate those denominators.

The remaining 55% remains subject to each worker's existing mandate and governing responsibilities. This change does not create a sales duty in every specialist role, transfer ownership, or authorize unsafe/unsupported commercial action. Each worker is measured on the attributable contribution available within its own mandate.

## 2. What earns the money-in KPI

Measure contribution through source-backed steps on the actual money path:
- a verified blocker removed from an existing buyer/payment/contract/invoice/settlement path;
- a current and qualified opportunity progressed to a substantive buyer response or requested next step;
- scoped work accepted, contract or invoice created, receivable progressed, payment received, or funds independently verified as withdrawable;
- a specific attributable contribution by a non-commercial worker that materially enables one of the above (for example, an authorized deployment, proof/access repair, acceptance test, integration, settlement verification or delivery artifact needed by a real buyer).

**Activity alone does not earn the outcome score.** A wake, long report, prompt edit, new candidate list, generic dashboard, raw message count, job listing, application draft, submitted application, nominal bounty amount, unaccepted deliverable or payment claim is not money-in. Preserve the canonical lifecycle:
DISCOVERED → QUALIFIED → OFFERED/SENT → RESPONDED → ACCEPTED → CONTRACTED → INVOICED/RECEIVABLE → PAID/SETTLED.

A worker may receive contribution credit for a verifiable necessary enabling step before cash arrives, but that must be separately reported as **attributable progress toward money-in**, not as realized income. Attribution must use evidence and must not count one result as multiple independent revenue events or credit one worker with an outcome it did not cause.

## 3. Relationship to the separate 60/20/20 opportunity-effort allocation

**KEEP THE RULES DISTINCT.**

- **45% = worker-performance/KPI weighting** applied Matrix-wide to each autonomous worker's attributable contribution toward verified money-in.
- **60/20/20 = effort allocation within the opportunity-search and conversion sourcing process**, as defined in the existing [Category A opportunity allocation and cost guard](https://github.com/amillimatrix-eng/CARBON-Creation-Hub/blob/main/AMX/ROOT/INTAKE/CATEGORY_A_OPPORTUNITY_ALLOCATION_AND_TAVILY_COST_GUARD_20261012.md).

The existing 60/20/20 rule continues to mean:
- 60% of sourcing/opportunity effort for real-value work such as direct buyer requests, pilots, builds, modernization, paid trials, short-term/remote contracts and explicit delivery requests;
- 20% for qualifying Micro-Matrix microtasking under the current threshold/settlement checks;
- 20% maximum for conventional platform/recruitment-led roles, after current verification and deduplication.

The 60/20/20 is not a Matrix-wide KPI and does not define 45% of any worker's score. The 45% KPI does not reallocate that sourcing split. Neither rule replaces the worker's registered mandate.

## 4. Inbound-offer and downstream-lifecycle exception to 60/20/20

The Owner clarifies that the 60/20/20 split is **not to be applied retroactively** after a sourcing allocation has already produced an opportunity or achieved its intended result.

**Inbound offers are exempt from the 60/20/20 sourcing-allocation filter.** Do not classify an inbound offer as outbound discovery merely to fit a percentage; do not delay, suppress, or put a received or accepted inbound offer into an artificial waiting lane because the conventional-role allocation is capped at 20%.

This especially covers the Owner-reported six inbound offers accepted from a set of ten examples: once an offer has arrived or been accepted, it is an existing opportunity/outcome requiring its own lifecycle handling—not another unit in the original sourcing-effort denominator. Those specific offers must be reconciled against current source records before asserting IDs or exact lifecycle states; this rule does not invent their identities or acceptance evidence.

For inbound/accepted opportunities, the existing iSCOPE→PRI chain remains in force according to the actual state:
- iSCOPE verifies source, fit, buyer/route and required qualification where still needed;
- already-qualified or accepted items move to PRI progression without duplicated discovery or repeated qualification;
- PRI advances real obligations: buyer-requested next step, scope, contract, invoice/receivable, delivery/acceptance, payment and follow-up;
- record current lifecycle, evidence/thread, owner, NEXT_ACTION and DUE/wait condition in the canonical commercial surface and read back;
- waiting for one buyer does not idle other executable work;
- inbound status is not assumed to mean accepted/contracted/paid. Each state still needs its own evidence.

The exception does not waive source verification, buyer fit, legal/platform rules, deliverability, compensation or settlement truth. It removes the irrelevant *sourcing-allocation cap/wait* from opportunities that are already inbound, received or accepted. A due inbound item must not be left waiting just because a 60/20/20 search lane is full.

## 5. Denominator, evidence and observability

The denominator is each worker's formally evaluated performance/KPI score. ROOT/T-GOV must define a consistent measurement method before claiming a numeric score has been applied. At minimum, the observable record for each relevant work episode must include:
- worker/office and active mandate;
- the actual commercial or money-path object and prior state;
- explicit problem/intent and evidence pointer;
- action actually taken, timestamp and exact output;
- attributable contribution and causal boundary (direct, enabling, shared, uncertain);
- state before/after and the strongest evidenced lifecycle stage;
- whether funds were received and withdrawable (Banker/provider/bank truth where applicable);
- remaining blocker, owner, NEXT_ACTION/DUE and acceptance evidence;
- selected KPI contribution class: VERIFIED_MONEY_IN / VERIFIED_RECEIVABLE_OR_ACCEPTANCE / ATTRIBUTABLE_ENABLING_PROGRESS / NO_MATERIAL_MONEY_PATH_CHANGE / UNKNOWN.

Use measured worker-level outcomes and receipts, not invented or manually fixed percentages. No attribution must be recorded as UNKNOWN rather than forced into a flattering or punitive conclusion.
