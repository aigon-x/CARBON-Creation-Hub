## Resolution of Intent record schema
Each tentacle/mandate record must contain:
- stable ID and canonical source/URL
- founding intent and expected observable result
- current owner/worker, execution surface, wake/run cadence, and last successful evidence timestamp
- upstream/downstream anchors and dependency links
- weights with provenance, rationale, confidence, decay/review rule, and selection consequence
- latest observed output and exact receipt/commit/deployment/CRM/payment references
- status: PASS / PARTIAL / FAIL / UNKNOWN / HOLD
- gap between intended and actual outcome, root cause, risk/severity, and whether drift is accidental or authorized
- next action, accountable owner, due/trigger condition, and acceptance test
- continuity handoff ID and supersession/reconciliation history

## Weighting rubric (initial, ROOT may revise)
Weights are decision weights, not decorative scores. Score each dimension 0–5 and attach evidence:
- Intent fidelity: 25%
- Runtime execution and reliability: 20%
- Verified outcome / money-in where relevant: 25%
- Anchoring and continuity: 15%
- Learning and next-selection improvement: 10%
- Cost, safety, and authority discipline: 5%

A high weighted score cannot override a critical failure: missing proof of money-in, broken authority boundary, silent schedule drift, unavailable critical runtime, or lost continuity forces FAIL/HOLD as appropriate. Do not score unavailable evidence as zero performance; mark UNKNOWN and name the retrieval step. Do not invent a composite until each dimension has a source.

## Acceptance tests
- A merge is not accepted as operational success until runtime adoption and intended consequence are demonstrated.
- A worker is not accepted as successful merely because it woke, ran, posted, or produced a candidate; its mandate-specific outcome must be evidenced.
- A sales route is not a win until buyer progress is evidenced; revenue success requires a verified paid/settled receipt.
- Every unresolved gap has an owner and next action; otherwise continuity is FAIL.
- Every material weight has provenance and an explicit effect on routing, prioritization, or selection; otherwise it is an unanchored annotation.
- New tentacles inherit the current canonical schema, weights, anchors, and handoff rules through versioned configuration/templates, with migration/readback tests and an explicit exception mechanism.
- Conflicting sources are reconciled; authority follows canonical governance, not the newest or loudest record.
- The report is read back from the persisted source before the flight can be called durably committed.
